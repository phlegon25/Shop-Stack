Questions

Frequently asked

How do I pay my subscription?

With MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money or bank transfer. Send the amount to the platform accounts shown inside the app, submit your reference, and a human verifies and activates your plan — usually within hours.

Does it work without internet?

The POS is designed for real Ghana conditions: keep selling when the network stutters, and everything syncs when you're back online.

Can my staff use it without seeing everything?

Yes. Roles control everything: cashiers sell, inventory clerks handle stock, accountants see reports, managers get almost everything. Every sensitive action is logged against the person who did it.

What happens when my trial ends?

Your data stays safe. You get a short grace period to pay, and the app reminds both you and our team. Renew and continue exactly where you stopped.

Is my data safe?

Your workspace is isolated per shop, sessions are short-lived tokens, passwords are Argon2id-hashed, and even our platform team cannot see your passwords. Platform admins act through audited, time-boxed sessions only.
